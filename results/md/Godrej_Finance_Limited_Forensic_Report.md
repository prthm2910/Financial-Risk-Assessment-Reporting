## {CID\_00006} Rationale and key rating drivers

{CID\_00007} CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has considered a consolidated approach of Godrej Capital Limited (GCL), the holding company of Godrej Housing Finance Limited (GHFL) and Godrej Finance Limited (GFL) for arriving at ratings of bank facilities, subordinate  debt  and  nonconvertible  debentures  of  GHFL.  Ratings  assigned  to  GHFL's  facilities/instruments  factor in  strong parentage of Godrej Industries Limited (GIL), which holds 90.7% stake in GCL (100% holding company of GHFL) as on March 31, 2025. Apart from the shared common brand name, GCL also derives support in terms of management and strategic linkages with GIL. Ratings also factor in expectation of continuous support by the ultimate parent towards its financial services business housed under GCL. Ratings derive comfort from the experienced board and management team, adequate capitalisation levels supported by continuous capital infusion by the parent, ability of GFL and GHFL to raise funds at competitive rates leveraging the group's relations with lenders and benefits derived from being part of the renowned group.

{CID\_00008} However, ratings remain constrained considering GCL's moderate profitability (on a consolidated basis) led by higher operatin g expenses due to upfront investment in technology and expansion, relatively moderate scale of operations and limited track record. Even though asset quality performance remains comfortable due to the limited track record and low seasoning of the portfolio, performance of asset quality through economic cycles is yet to be established.

## {CID\_00027} Detailed description of key rating drivers:

## {CID\_00028} Key strengths

## {CID\_00029} Strong parentage with demonstrated support and integration of the group

{CID\_00030} Godrej Group forayed in the financial services sector through its subsidiary, GCL, which held 90.7% stake as on March 31, 2025. GCL conducts its business through its subsidiaries, GHFL and GFL. As the flagship company of the Godrej Group, GIL has a promoter stake of 69.65% as on March 31, 2025. Ratings are supported by strong operational, managerial, and financial linkages of GCL to the Godrej Group, and the shared brand identity. GIL is envisaged to provide capital support to the financial services business, which has been strategically prioritised for growth within the group. GIL aims to maintain at least 51% shareholding, ensuring ongoing control over GCL. GCL benefits from managerial support within the group, particularly from Pirojsha Godrej, who serves as non-executive director at GFL and GHFL and holding positions on the boards of group companies. GCL takes advantage of the Godrej ecosystem, which allows it to secure competitive funding rates. The robust brand equity that GFL and GH FL possess as part of the Godrej Group, and positive customer acceptance, bodes well for the company's growth potential.

## {CID\_00031} Experienced management team

{CID\_00032} GCL's operations are led by Manish Shah, the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer. With over 22 years of experience in financial services, Shah has worked in sectors including secured and unsecured lending, credit cards, wealth management, and life insurance. He is supported by a professional management team with expertise in credit underwriting, technology, strategy, and other relevant areas Leveraging the extensive experience of its board and management, the company has established a robust credit underwriting and risk management framework to ensure the sustained scalability of its business operations.

## {CID\_00033} Adequate capitalisation supported by regular equity infusions from parent

{CID\_00034} GCL's capitalisation profile stands adequate bolstered by consistent equity infusion from its parent company. Till date, the Godrej Group has infused ~₹3000 crore in GCL. Of this infusion, ~₹950 crore was infused in FY24, and ₹800 crore was infused in H1FY25. On a consolidated basis, GCL's capital levels remained adequate with tangible net worth (TNW) of ₹3,021 crore as on March 31, 2025  (March  31,  2024:  ₹2,088  crore). The  capital  adequacy  ratio  (CAR)  for  GHFL  and  GFL  stood  at  20.69%  and  23.09%, respectively, as on March 31, 2025 , exceeding regulatory requirement. GCL's gearing stood at 4.73x as on March 31, 2025, against 4.08x as on March 31, 2024. CareEdge Ratings envisages gearing (on a consolidated basis) to increase to 6x on a steady state basis, in the near term. CareEdge Ratings believes GCL will continue to receive ongoing support from the group for growth capital and in times of need.

## {CID\_00035} Ability to raise funds at competitive rates

{CID\_00036} As on March 31, 2025, GCL's resource profile continues to remain moderately diversified with 65.6% borrowings as term loans from banks, 0.5% in the form of National Housing Bank (NHB) borrowings, 5.9% from foreign banks and the remaining 28.0% in the form of market borrowings (CPs and NCDs). The company's ability to diversify its resource base has been bolstered by its affiliation  with  the  Godrej  Group,  which  provides  significant  financial  flexibility,  and  demonstrating  ability  to  secure  funds  at competitive rates and foster strong relationships with lenders. Even though the company has been able to raise funds at favourable borrowing rates compared to its peers, GCL experienced rise in the overall cost of borrowings in FY25 compared to last year, mainly due to rising interest rate scenario. Going forward, GCL's ability to further diversify its resource profile while eff ectively managing its asset-liability profile, would be a key rating monitorable.

## {CID\_00037} Key weaknesses

## {CID\_00038} Moderate scale of operations and limited seasoning

{CID\_00039} GCL was established in September 2019, while GHFL began its business operations in November 2020, followed by GFL in March 2022. Consequently, these entities continue to be in nascent stages of their business operations. GCL's assets under management (AUM) stood at ₹ 16929 crore as on March 31, 2025, against ₹ 10761 crore as on March 31, 2024, increasing 57% on a y-o-y basis. Total disbursements also saw a significant growth of 49% y-oy from ₹7406 crore in FY24 to ₹11042 crore in FY25. GCL's loan portfolio remains relatively less seasoned since majority disbursements occurred in the last two years. GCL's asset quality o n a combined basis remains healthy with GS3 (90+ days past due (dpd; %) at 0.35% (March 31, 2024: 0.10%). However, due to the moderate track record and low seasoning driven by high growth rates, performance of asset quality over economic cycles is yet to be fully established.
